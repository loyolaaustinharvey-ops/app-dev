# *Supernatural*

### This series is about two brothers who go out and hunt different urban legends and mythological monsters all throughout America.

### It revolves around the family of the Winchesters which are named "Mary" the mother, "John" the father, and the two brothers "Sam" and "Dean".

### The way that the show works is by telling it through episodes where each passing episode takes the brothers to different parts of America which in turn makes them also hunt different monsters within different regions.


# *Monsters*
